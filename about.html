About Us

Built by Marathoners, Grounded in Peer-Reviewed Sports Science

RunPaceStudio is a mobile-first progressive web application (PWA) engineered for marathon runners, track athletes, coaches, and everyday treadmill users who want laboratory-grade pacing accuracy without intrusive pop-ups, account walls, or bloated mobile apps.

When you are mid-workout at 88% of your maximum heart rate with sweat on your fingertips, you do not have time to do mental math between miles per hour (MPH), kilometers per hour (km/h), incline grades (0% to 15%), and outdoor Grade Adjusted Pace (GAP). We built RunPaceStudio with oversized 48px+ touch targets, instant MILE ↔ KM unit switching, and real-time metabolic calculations so you can focus 100% on your stride.

Our Scientific Methodology & Citations

Every formula inside RunPaceStudio is cross-checked against published exercise physiology literature. Where runners rely on practical community heuristics (such as temperature + dew point tables or the MAF 180 rule), we clearly label them as rules of thumb alongside their peer-reviewed context:

  • 1% Treadmill Equivalence Rule: Jones, A. M., & Doust, J. H. (1996). A 1% treadmill grade most accurately reflects the energetic cost of outdoor running. Journal of Sports Sciences, 14(4), 321–327.
  • Running Oxygen Cost & Calorie Equations: American College of Sports Medicine (ACSM). ACSM's Guidelines for Exercise Testing and Prescription ($\text{VO}_2 = 0.2v + 0.9vG + 3.5\text{ ml}\cdot\text{kg}^{-1}\cdot\text{min}^{-1}$).
  • Steep Grade Energy Cost (0–15%+ Incline): Minetti, A. E., Moia, C., Roi, G. S., Susta, D., & Ferretti, G. (2002). Energy cost of walking and running at extreme uphill and downhill slopes. Journal of Applied Physiology, 93(3), 1039–1046.
  • VDOT & Training Zone Prescriptions: Daniels, J., & Gilbert, J. (1979). Oxygen Power: Performance Tables for Distance Runners; Daniels, J. Daniels' Running Formula.
  • Speed-Specific Running Biomechanics: Weyand, P. G., et al. (2000). Faster top running speeds are achieved with greater ground forces not more rapid leg movements. Journal of Applied Physiology, 89(5), 1991–1999; Heiderscheit, B. C., et al. (2011). Effects of step rate manipulation on joint mechanics during running. Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise.
  • Aerodynamic Wind & Thermal Pacing Models: Pugh, L. G. C. E. (1971). The influence of wind resistance in running and walking and the mechanical efficiency of work against horizontal or vertical forces. The Journal of Physiology; Davies, C. T. M. (1980). Effects of wind assistance and resistance on the forward motion of a runner. Journal of Applied Physiology; Ely, M. R., et al. (2007). Impact of weather on marathon-running performance.

Privacy-First & Offline-Ready Architecture

RunPaceStudio operates 100% inside your browser. Your pace preferences, workout cue sheets, and body-weight settings are saved only in your own device's localStorage and are never transmitted to any external database. Once loaded, our Service Worker caches the application so it continues working reliably in basement gyms or marathon start corrals with weak cellular reception.
