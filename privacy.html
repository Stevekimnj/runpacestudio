Privacy Policy

Your Privacy & Data Protection

Last Updated: October 2026

At RunPaceStudio, we believe your training data belongs strictly to you. Our application is designed as a client-side utility that calculates treadmill paces, incline equivalents, workout cue sheets, and race splits directly inside your device's web browser.

1. Zero Server-Side Personal Data Collection

We do not require user accounts, logins, or email registrations to use RunPaceStudio. Any numbers you enter—such as your running pace, race finish times, age for the MAF 180 calculator, or body weight for calorie estimation—are processed exclusively on your device and stored locally in your browser's localStorage under the key rps.v2 so your preferences are remembered on your next visit. You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser site data.

2. Cookies & Google AdSense Advertising

RunPaceStudio itself does not set tracking cookies in its core calculator. When advertising is enabled via Google AdSense, third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to RunPaceStudio and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by visiting www.aboutads.info.

3. Amazon Associates & External Links

RunPaceStudio participates in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com. External links to YouTube biomechanics videos and Amazon gear pages include rel="noopener noreferrer" security attributes. Once you click an external link, you are subject to the privacy policy of that destination website.

4. GDPR & CCPA Rights

Because we do not store personal data on our servers, we do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties. Visitors from the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and California have the right to manage browser storage and cookie consent preferences directly through their browser settings or consent banner controls.
